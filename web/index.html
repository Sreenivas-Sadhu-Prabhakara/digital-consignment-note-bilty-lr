Consignment note register for road transport offices

Issue a numbered consignment note — the bilty, the lorry receipt — in its three conventional copies, each carrying what that party needs and not what it does not. Track the consignment from booked to delivered and raise a proof of delivery from the record. The number series is audited on every read: a gap or a reused number is said out loud, because a register whose numbers have holes in it is not evidence of anything.

        Cancel this note

        A cancelled note keeps its number for good. That is what stops a hole appearing in the series.

        Renumber this note

        The only reason to renumber is that two notes ended up on one number. The way out is forwards, to the end of the series — moving one backwards into a gap would put the register out of date order as well.

         

        The office, the series and the figures

        Entered once. The office's own details go on every copy; the series is what the numbers are audited in; the figures are what the tax position is worked out on, and they are indicative and yours to change.

        The number series

        A transport office keeps one series per booking office, or one per financial year, or both. The first number of a series that already has notes in it cannot be moved: the audit measures the first gap from it.
